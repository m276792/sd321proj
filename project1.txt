Examining the Relationship between carbon emissions and climate events

Our dataset looks at variables such as economic impact, carbon emissions, country and region.

Question:
Are countries with the highest carbon emissions affected by climate events, or is the relationship inversely proportional?

URLs:
Global Economic Indicators:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/tanishksharma9905/global-economic-indicators-20102025
Global Climate Events and Economic Impact:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/uom190346a/global-climate-events-and-economic-impact-dataset?select=global_climate_events_economic_impact_2020_2025.csv
Emissions by Country:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/thedevastator/global-fossil-co2-emissions-by-country-2002-2022

More questions:
1. How are countries economically impacted by climate events, as countries and as regions?
2. Have carbon emissions continued to grow annually or plateaued?
3. What is the relationship between emissions and economic strength?
4. What are further factors we can research to find more relationships?
5. With countries that experience frequent climate events, is the economic impact of those events constant or does it grow?
6. Is the frequency of climate events growing over time?

We will divide the work by splitting up our original questions and creating SQL statements to examine those. 
We will meet regularly to decide on our next actions, and use github to see updates on our work.
